# Café Order Management Prototype

## Project Option
**Option Chosen:** Café (Order Management App)

**Reasoning:** A café is far more interesting than a salon due to the many variables they may have to manage. Creating a way to manage the orders does not only mean managing what the clients ask, but also it could add seasonal specials, or always take into account what is available in the storage.

## Problem Statement
A small Cuban café currently manages all in-store and online orders manually without a unified tracking system. This causes order delays and customer confusion for both the kitchen staff and the customers waiting for food. Our prototype will streamline the ordering process by providing a unified dashboard that tracks an order's preparation stage and alerts the customer when it is ready so that the café can handle high volumes without delays, and customers always know exactly when to expect their order.

## Personas

### Persona 1: The Customer
* **Name:** Mateo Ramirez
* **Role:** Local Customer (Office worker on his lunch break)
* **Summary:** Mateo has a short 30-minute lunch break and wants to order quickly to avoid long lines. His main frustration is standing around not knowing if his order is being made or forgotten. He is highly comfortable with technology and uses mobile ordering regularly. 
* **Quote:** *"I just want to grab my lunch without spending half of my break standing in a line wondering when my food will be ready."*

### Persona 2: The Staff Member
* **Name:** Elena Gonzalez
* **Role:** Shift Supervisor / Cashier at the café
* **Summary:** Elena wants to keep the kitchen running smoothly during the lunch rush and keep customers happy. Currently, paper tickets get lost or smudged, and she is constantly interrupted by customers asking about their food. She is moderately comfortable with technology and needs a simple, fast system without unnecessary complexity.
* **Quote:** *"When the lunch rush hits, I can't be running back and forth to the kitchen just to check if an order is done—I need to see it all on one screen."*

## Scope

### Must-Have Features (In Prototype)
1. **Unified Order Dashboard:** A central screen (like a tablet) for staff that shows all incoming orders (both in-store and online) in one place.
2. **Order Status Tracking (Kanban style):** The ability for staff to move an order through specific stages (e.g., "Received" → "Preparing" → "Ready").
3. **Customer Order Status Screen:** A simple digital display screen (or a simple web link) where customers can see if their order is being prepared or ready for pickup.
4. **Menu Browsing & Item Selection:** A basic interface for the customer to view the café's menu and select items.
5. **Simple Order Submission:** The ability for the customer to place an order and have it instantly appear on the staff's dashboard.

### Features Cut / Not in this Prototype
* **SMS Notification:** Getting a text message informing you when your order is done. (Cut: Good, but may be unnecessary or redundant for an early prototype).
* **Order Modifications:** Allows leaving a comment or giving special instructions regarding the order. (Cut until the prototype proves functional).
* **In-App Online Payments:** The prototype will only handle placing the order. Customers will still pay at the register when they pick up (to keep the prototype simple and avoid complex payment gateway integration).
